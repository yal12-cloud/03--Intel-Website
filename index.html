Sustainability Through the Ages

Explore Intel's journey through time and sustainability.

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename the newly formed company NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel releases the 4004, the world’s first commercial microprocessor, marking the beginning of the microprocessor era and transforming the future of computing.

1978

8086 Processor

Intel launches the 8086 processor, introducing the x86 architecture that became the foundation for personal computers and enterprise systems worldwide.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, bringing improved performance, multitasking capabilities, and advancing modern computing.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel reaches peak greenhouse gas emissions and begins investing heavily in renewable energy, energy efficiency, and sustainable manufacturing practices.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches the RISE strategy, focusing on Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, and Enabling goals to create long-term positive impact for society.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel announces its commitment to achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions across global operations by 2040, supporting global climate goals.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel achieves 99% renewable electricity use across worldwide operations, significantly reducing emissions and supporting a cleaner energy future.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit, bringing together suppliers, government leaders, and innovators to accelerate sustainability solutions.
